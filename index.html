About BPSC TRE

The Bihar Public Service Commission runs the Teacher Recruitment Examination (TRE) in successive phases to fill teaching posts across Bihar's government schools, from Primary (Class 1–5) right up through Higher Secondary (Class 11–12). Each phase carries its own set of question papers, but the overall exam structure, marking scheme, and subject areas stay broadly consistent from one phase to the next, which makes older papers genuinely useful practice material for the next round.

TRE 1.0 — the first recruitment phase TRE 2.0 — expanded subject coverage TRE 3.0 — most recent completed phase

The papers on this page are organized by phase and by class level so you can quickly find the exact paper you need, and every download is counted so you can see which papers other candidates are finding most useful.

TRE 4.0 — What's Confirmed So Far

BPSC opened the fourth recruitment phase with a notification issued on 18 August 2026 under Advertisement No. 14/2026, covering roughly 32,388 teaching posts. Online applications are open from 1 September through 30 September 2026, with the written exam tentatively scheduled for 22–27 September 2026 — though candidates should treat that exam window as provisional until BPSC confirms it closer to the date.
